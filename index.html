Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in Dokvio-PDF Scan & Doc Viewer.

Information Collection and Use

Most features of Dokvio-PDF Scan & Doc Viewer (such as document scanning, PDF reading, Word, and DOCX document viewing) operate entirely offline on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit local files or personal data to our own servers.

Camera and Storage Permissions

The app requests camera access solely to allow you to scan physical documents. It requests storage access only to save, read, and view PDF, Word, and DOCX files locally on your device. These permissions are used exclusively for core app functionality.

Cloud Processing & Online Viewer (PPTX Files)

To provide online viewing and processing features for presentation formats (such as PPTX files), files chosen by the user for online viewing may be transmitted to secure cloud storage infrastructure (Cloudinary). This data transmission is strictly used to render and provide the viewing feature and is encrypted in transit.

Future Advertisements (AdMob)

We may integrate third-party ad networks, such as Google AdMob, in future updates to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect device identifiers or ad-tracking data in accordance with Google’s privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
